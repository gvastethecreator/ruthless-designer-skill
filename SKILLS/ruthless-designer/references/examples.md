## Pattern Contraindications And Less Obvious Signatures

Treat patterns as bets, not kits. Artifact stage: one object under sustained inspection; avoid for peer comparison. Evidence pane: claims beside proof; avoid when evidence is sparse or repeats the primary surface. Split workspace: one action across two regions; avoid when one side becomes permanent navigation.

Use an **instrument panel** when presets express intent, live controls shape an output, and diagnostics explain behavior without dominating it. Keep the output sovereign. Avoid when a short form plus result would do the job; a cockpit built for three inputs is cosplay with knobs.

Look beyond hero treatments:

- **Timeline as control:** history, forecast, or sequence is directly scrubbed, edited, or compared.
- **Recovery rail:** failed, deferred, or reversible work stays visible beside main path instead of vanishing into toast purgatory.
- **Command surface:** expert actions, scope, and consequences share one inspectable execution layer.

A signature is valid only when it changes comprehension or action.
